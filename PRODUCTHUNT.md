## 📄 Description longue (pour le post de lancement)

> **The problem:** Most kanji apps either cost $10/month, gamify with aggressive streaks, or overwhelm you with 2000 kanji from day one.
>
> **What I built:** Asano Kanji gives you exactly 10 kanji every morning — with on/kun readings, vocabulary examples, and a short quiz. It tracks what you've learned with spaced repetition, and stores your personal word list across all your devices.
>
> **What makes it different:**
> - Completely free to use daily. No subscription required.
> - No streak freeze gimmicks — it just shows your real consistency, honestly.
> - Works as a PWA (install on iPhone or Android, no App Store needed)
> - JLPT levels N5 to N1 — go at your own pace
> - 5 languages: EN, FR, ES, DE, RU
> - Syncs across devices via your account
>
> I built this because I was learning Japanese myself and couldn't find anything simple and free. It started as a side project, and now I use it every morning.
>
> Would love your feedback — especially from anyone studying for JLPT!
